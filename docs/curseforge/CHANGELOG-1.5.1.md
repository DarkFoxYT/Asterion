# Asterion 1.5.1

Maintenance update for Asterion 1.5.

- Includes the updated bundled Amnetic renderer.
- Includes preset file-handling safety repairs.

Available for Minecraft 1.20.1, 1.21.1, and 26.1.2, with Fabric/Quilt,
Forge, and NeoForge variants. GeckoLib is required. Fabric and Quilt
builds also require Fabric API.
